## Professional Self-Assessment

Throughout my time in the Computer Science program, I have developed a strong foundation in software development, problem-solving, and system design. Completing this ePortfolio has allowed me to reflect on my growth and showcase the skills I have built across multiple areas, including software engineering, algorithms and data structures, and database development.

One of the most important skills I developed is the ability to design and improve software systems. In my mobile application project, I enhanced both the structure and user experience by refactoring code, improving the interface, and adding validation and categorization features. This experience showed me how important clean code and user-focused design are when building applications.

I also strengthened my understanding of algorithms and data structures. In my CS 300 project, I improved efficiency by replacing unnecessary sorting operations with a map data structure, reducing time complexity and improving performance. This project helped me understand how choosing the right data structure can significantly impact the effectiveness of a solution.

In addition, I gained experience working with databases and full-stack applications. In my CS 340 project, I transformed a read-only dashboard into a full CRUD system with filtering, sorting, and data visualization. This demonstrated my ability to connect backend database logic with a user interface and create applications that support decision-making.

Throughout the program, I also developed important professional skills such as problem-solving, debugging, and adapting to challenges. Many times, code did not work as expected, and I had to troubleshoot errors, rethink my approach, and find solutions. These experiences helped me become more confident and prepared for real-world development work.

This ePortfolio represents my growth as a computer science student and demonstrates my ability to design, develop, and improve software systems. It also reflects my readiness to enter the field and continue learning as a software developer.
